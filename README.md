Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Five Seconds to Explain the Glass"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-five-seconds-to-explain-the-glass-934d9949?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=u-po-pu-five-seconds-to-explain-the-glass-934d9949): a house cat gets five seconds to explain why it knocked a glass off the table — and it is not apologising.

## The line

> "I gave that glass three days to move out of my sunspot and it refused, so I evicted it the only way the lease allows."

**117 characters, one sentence.**

Why it fits the judge brief: it is a first-person declaration in the cat's own voice, not a human apology — the cat is the landlord of the sunspot and the glass is the tenant who ignored its notice. The excuse opens with a bold claim (three days' notice given) and undercuts it with a defiant eviction, so it carries two beats of cat logic with zero remorse and no confession. The details are concrete and specific to the cat's world — three days, a sunspot, a lease — instead of the obvious gravity or slippery-edge lines, and the whole thing is a spoken line a cat could plausibly deliver in five seconds, with every character earning its place.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after validating it is a single sentence of 1–200 characters with no leading or trailing whitespace, then verifies the write by reading it back.
